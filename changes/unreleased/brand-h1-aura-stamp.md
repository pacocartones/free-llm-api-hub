### Added

- Every provider now has an *aura*: a small circle drawn from its own record at build time. Its colours are the
  modalities it serves, a full disc means an ongoing free tier and a ring a trial, a dashed edge means its docs leave
  a production flag unsaid, and it fades when the last check is over 30 days old. It leads each row of the explorer
  and sits at the top of each provider page with its reading in words; the methodology explains how to read it.
- The verified date is now a *receipt* stamp ("✓ 24 Nov · source") that links to the page the entry was checked on;
  hover it to read the sentence quoted from that page. Due and stale entries keep their own mark and border.
- New brand mark: the hub's own aura, in the header, the favicon, the app icons and `assets/logo-*.svg`.
