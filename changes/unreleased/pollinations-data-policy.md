### Added

- Pollinations.ai (`pollinations`): prompt data policy recorded (#744).
  Its privacy policy says prompts and outputs are not used to train or
  fine-tune models without explicit opt-in, and are processed transiently,
  with generated response caches typically kept up to 30 days. The
  no-training statement covers Pollinations itself: requests sent to an
  externally hosted community model follow that provider's own practices.
