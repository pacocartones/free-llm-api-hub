### Changed and fixed

- Accessibility pass (November 2026). The home page's hero, `<h1>`, first-use
  strip and explorer filters are now inside `<main>`, so "jump to main" lands
  on the heading and no content sits outside a landmark. Source links in the
  guide tables, the speed lines on compare pages and the source under a
  provider's quote are underlined, not told apart by colour alone (WCAG
  1.4.1). On phones, opening the menu moves keyboard focus into it; Escape in
  the Providers menu returns focus to "Providers" instead of a hidden link.
  The theme button is named "Light theme" with a pressed state, and the two
  tables on `/coding-tools/` have distinct names. A new test
  (`scripts/a11y-guard.test.mjs`) checks these on every built page (#959).
