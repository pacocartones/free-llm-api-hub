### Added

- BytePlus ModelArk (`byteplus-modelark`): a one-time free inference quota for
  new BytePlus users of 500,000 tokens per model, counted separately for each
  model and shared across the primary account, on the OpenAI-compatible
  `https://ark.ap-southeast.bytepluses.com/api/v3` (Seed, DeepSeek, GLM and
  other models). It covers online token-billed inference only. An account that
  has not activated model services can turn on Free Tokens Only mode, which
  pauses the service instead of billing once the free tokens are used up. The
  campaign terms require enterprise information, allow one redemption per
  account and say the quota may have a validity period. Card, phone,
  commercial use and the prompt data policy are not stated on the pages that
  could be read. Checked 2026-11-27, agent-assisted.

With this addition, 30 of 96 providers (31.3%) list no `text` modality, under
the 35% non-text guardrail.
