## The three changes that matter most

1. **{{removed_count}} providers left the list and {{added_count}} joined.** {{removed_names}} are out; {{added_names}} are in. The dataset goes from {{providers_before}} to {{providers_after}} providers.
2. **SambaNova's free tier shrank and is now evaluation-only.** The Free Tier lists two models (gpt-oss-120b and gemma-4-31B-it) instead of three, and its docs now say all SambaCloud models are Preview models for evaluation, not production. We record `commercial_ok: false`.

## What changed for existing providers

Each line is **before → after**, with the day we read the provider's page and a link to it. Where the repository doesn't say whether the provider changed its terms or our earlier reading was incomplete, we write "now recorded as".

### Less free, or more restricted

- **SambaNova Cloud** — Free Tier models: DeepSeek-V3.1, Llama-3.3-70B, gpt-oss-120b → gpt-oss-120b and gemma-4-31B-it, each still 20 requests/min, 20 requests/day and 200,000 tokens/day. Commercial use: allowed → **not allowed**: "All SambaCloud models are Preview models: they are intended for evaluation purposes and developer experimentation only". Checked 24 Nov. [Source](https://docs.sambanova.ai/docs/en/models/rate-limits) · [entry](https://freellmapihub.com/p/sambanova)
- **Datalab** — $20/month (work email) or $10/month (personal email) free allowance → **one-time credit**: $10 at signup plus $10 after adding a card with a work email ($5 + $5 with a personal email). "The credit does not expire." It moves from ongoing to trial. Rate limits now recorded as 25 requests/min and 25 concurrent (we had 10 and 5). Checked 24 Nov. [Source](https://documentation.datalab.to/platform/billing) · [entry](https://freellmapihub.com/p/datalab)
- **Alibaba Cloud Model Studio** — qwen-plus, qwen-flash and qwen-turbo **no longer carry a free quota** for new users. The new-user quota is still typically 1,000,000 tokens per model, Singapore region only. Last verified 22 Nov. [Source](https://www.alibabacloud.com/help/en/model-studio/new-free-quota) · [entry](https://freellmapihub.com/p/alibaba-model-studio)
- **Speechify** — Simba 3.5: 500K characters/month → **333K characters/month from 21 November 2026**. Simba 3.0 and 3.2 stay at 500K. Last verified 20 Nov. [Source](https://speechify.ai/pricing) · [entry](https://freellmapihub.com/p/speechify)
- **AssemblyAI** — the $50 signup credit covered speech to text, Speech Understanding and the LLM Gateway → **the LLM Gateway is excluded**. Its gateway also left our free client exports (v2.14.0). Last verified 20 Nov. [Source](https://www.assemblyai.com/pricing) · [entry](https://freellmapihub.com/p/assemblyai)
- **Novita** — the pricing page listed a few LLMs as Free (Ling 3.1 Flash, Apodex 1.1 Mini) → **no LLM on the pricing page is priced at $0**. The 90-day validity of the Sandbox credits is no longer stated, so `expires` is unset. Last verified 20 Nov. [Source](https://novita.ai/pricing) · [entry](https://freellmapihub.com/p/novita)
- **Veryfi** — Free Forever plan included structured extraction → **Document to JSON is not included on Free**; still 100 documents/month. Last verified 19 Nov. [Source](https://www.veryfi.com/pricing/) · [entry](https://freellmapihub.com/p/veryfi)
- **Nutrient** — 5,000 credits/month → **$5 of usage every month**, shared across all APIs, no rollover. Commercial use: not stated → conditional ("limited commercial use without a wallet" on Free). Last verified 19 Nov. [Source](https://www.nutrient.io/api/pricing/data-extraction-api/) · [entry](https://freellmapihub.com/p/nutrient)
- **Pollinations** — since **1 November 2026** a `pk_` key only identifies an app, and requests that send one get HTTP 401; generation needs a secret key. Commercial use: not stated → conditional: "Model licences vary; verify before commercial use." Last verified 20 Nov. [Source](https://gen.pollinations.ai/docs) · [entry](https://freellmapihub.com/p/pollinations)
- **Google Gemini API (AI Studio)** — now recorded: API access to the 2.5 models is **limited to users who actively used them before** (no start date given), and Gemini 2.5 Pro TTS has no free tier. Read 24 Nov. [Models](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/models) · [pricing](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/pricing) · [entry](https://freellmapihub.com/p/google-gemini)
- **AI21 Labs** — commercial use: not stated → **not allowed**: its Terms of Use make a free trial an "Evaluation Product" for internal evaluation only (§5.2, §5.3). Checked in November (v2.14.2). [Source](https://docs.ai21.com/docs/usage-cost) · [entry](https://freellmapihub.com/p/ai21)
- **Photoroom** — commercial use: not stated → **not allowed**: its terms license Free Accounts "for your own personal, non commercial purposes". Checked 24 Nov. [Source](https://www.photoroom.com/legal/terms-and-conditions) · [entry](https://freellmapihub.com/p/photoroom)
- **Cerebras** — commercial use: allowed → **not allowed** for the free credit: the pricing page marks the self-serve Developer tier, which carries the free $5 credit, "For development, evaluation, and experimentation; not intended for production use." Checked 26 Nov. [Source](https://www.cerebras.ai/pricing) · [entry](https://freellmapihub.com/p/cerebras)
- **Google Gemini API (AI Studio)** — commercial use: allowed → **conditional**: business use is allowed, but the Gemini API terms say API clients offered to users in the EEA, Switzerland or the UK may use only paid services, and unpaid quota is not one. Checked 27 Nov. [Source](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms) · [entry](https://freellmapihub.com/p/google-gemini)
- **Jina AI** — commercial use: allowed → **conditional**: the service terms cover commercial use of the hosted API, except jina-embeddings-v4, which is under the Qwen Research License (research and non-commercial use only, through the API too). Checked 27 Nov. [Source](https://jina.ai/embeddings/) · [entry](https://freellmapihub.com/p/jina-ai)
- **Unreal Speech** — commercial use: allowed → **conditional**: "Yes, audio generated with Unreal Speech can be used commercially", but Free plan users must credit Unreal Speech with a link to unrealspeech.com. Checked 26 Nov. [Source](https://unrealspeech.com/) · [entry](https://freellmapihub.com/p/unreal-speech)

### More free, or clearer

- **Rime** — one-time free minutes (the pricing card said ~800, the FAQ and docs 3,000) → **$50 of free usage every month** across every Rime model, no card, no rollover. It moves from trial to ongoing. Last verified 19 Nov. [Source](https://rime.ai/pricing) · [entry](https://freellmapihub.com/p/rime)
- **Gladia** — €50 signup credit with no stated validity → **valid for one year**. Last verified 19 Nov. [Source](https://www.gladia.io/pricing) · [entry](https://freellmapihub.com/p/gladia)
- **Contextual AI** — $25 in free credits → $25, or **$50 with a work email**. Commercial use: not stated → **allowed**: its terms grant the right to make Customer Applications available to third parties, with no evaluation-only clause. Checked 24 Nov. [Source](https://contextual.ai/pricing/) · [entry](https://freellmapihub.com/p/contextual-ai)
- **Fish Audio** — the s2.1-pro-free TTS model was $0 "through 2026-11-30" under fair-use limits → still $0, and **the docs no longer state an end date** or fair-use limits. Last verified 20 Nov. [Source](https://fish.audio/plan/) · [entry](https://freellmapihub.com/p/fish-audio)
- **Twelve Labs** — the Analyze API now has published Free plan limits: **1,000 requests/day, 60/min**, next to the Embed limits. Last verified 20 Nov. [Source](https://docs.twelvelabs.io/docs/get-started/rate-limits) · [entry](https://freellmapihub.com/p/twelve-labs)
- **Upstage** — Solar Mini 4 at Tier 0: 50,000 tokens/min → **250,000 tokens/min** (50,000 on legacy tiers). Last verified 19 Nov. [Source](https://console.upstage.ai/docs/getting-started) · [entry](https://freellmapihub.com/p/upstage)
- **Cloudflare Workers AI** — `@cf/google/gemma-4-26b-a4b-it`, a free-access model on the pricing page, joins the sample. `@cf/meta/llama-3.1-8b-instruct` and `@cf/google/gemma-3-12b-it` are gone from the catalogue and leave it. Model tier 2 → 3. Checked 24 Nov. [Source](https://developers.cloudflare.com/workers-ai/platform/pricing/) · [entry](https://freellmapihub.com/p/cloudflare-workers-ai)
- **Speechmatics** — now one $100 balance across Speech-to-Text, add-ons and Text-to-Speech. Last verified 20 Nov. [Source](https://www.speechmatics.com/pricing)
- **Cartesia** — 20,000 credits/month, plus a separate **$1/month Managed Agents allowance**. Last verified 19 Nov. [Source](https://cartesia.ai/pricing)
- **Jina AI** — 10M free tokens "one-time" → **10M free tokens per new API key**. Last verified 20 Nov. [Source](https://jina.ai/embeddings/)
- **Numbers now published and recorded:** Moondream 2 requests/second on the Base Tier, the default for all keys ([source](https://docs.moondream.ai/rate-limits), 24 Nov); AI Horde 2 requests/second and 90/min per IP on submit routes ([source](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Haidra-Org/AI-Horde/main/docs/reference/service_limits.md), 24 Nov); CAMB.AI API concurrency 1 on Free ([source](https://www.camb.ai/pricing), 19 Nov); Mixedbread 100 queries/min and 6,000 uploads/min ([source](https://www.mixedbread.com/pricing), 19 Nov); NLP Cloud 2 parallel requests on Free ([source](https://nlpcloud.com/pricing.html), 20 Nov).

## New in the dataset this month

- **`field_checks`: why a yes/no flag holds its value** (#719). For the card, phone, commercial-use and OpenAI-compatibility flags we record the pages read, the day, and either the provider's sentence or why the flag stays "not stated": the pages don't say (`source_silent`), only an account would tell (`needs_account`), or two official pages disagree (`sources_disagree`). {{field_checks_after}} checks on {{field_checks_providers_after}} providers so far: {{field_checks_stated_after}} stated, {{field_checks_silent_after}} silent, {{field_checks_account_after}} need an account, {{field_checks_disagree_after}} disagree.
- **`anonymous_access`** (#731, #737): APIs you can call with no account, each with the sentence that says so. {{anonymous_count_after}} so far: {{anonymous_names}}. See the [no-account collection](https://freellmapihub.com/collections/anonymous-access).
- **`published_speed`** (#728, #733): a speed figure only when the provider publishes it for a model in its free sample, with its exact words. {{speed_count_after}} providers so far: {{speed_names}}. These are vendor claims, not our measurements, and no provider says they apply to the free tier, so there is no "fastest" ranking.
- **`commercial_ok: "conditional"`**, with the condition in words, first used this month: {{commercial_conditional_after}} providers so far.
- **`models_url`** and **`playground_url`**: official model catalogues ({{models_url_after}} providers) and provider-owned "Try it" links ({{playground_names}}).
- **New pages:** one page per model, `/models/<model>/` (#711); a public [data quality page](https://freellmapihub.com/quality/) (#765); a [Not free page](https://freellmapihub.com/not-free/) with the provider's own words (#787); and the data is now mirrored to Hugging Face and Kaggle on each release (#716).

## What we couldn't confirm

The dataset says "not stated" rather than guess. These are the open questions from {{month_name}}:

- **Poolside:** does free hosted inference still work after the announced 31 October shutdown? Its own pages disagree.
- **NVIDIA NIM:** free credits for prototyping are confirmed, but no credit amount, rate limit or expiry is published, and the trial terms page we had cited now returns 404. The 40 requests/min figure we listed is no longer stated.
- **Credit amounts no longer published:** Upstage (the $10 figure is gone from its docs and pricing page), Baseten (no amount or expiry), W&B Inference (no Free amount published).
- **Gemini:** the free-tier numbers for the 2.5 models appear only inside AI Studio, after sign-in.
- **Training policy, conflicting pages:** Rime (its 29 October 2026 terms permit training on Free Plan data, while its older privacy policy says no training without agreement; we removed its data-policy row until this is settled), Gladia (pricing, security pages and Compliance Hub disagree), Mixedbread (privacy policy and terms disagree).
- **Smaller gaps:** Unreal Speech doesn't say whether its 250,000 characters are monthly; Voyage AI's pricing page is inconsistent about which rerankers get 200M free tokens; Contextual AI doesn't say which APIs its credits cover; Rime's concurrency limit on Free is unpublished; OVHcloud's "Free" model labels could not be read.
- **No sample of free models yet:** NLP Cloud, Contextual AI, Upstage and Novita. No official page ties their free credits to model IDs.
