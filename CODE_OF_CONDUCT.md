# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

This project exists to give developers accurate, useful information. Keeping it welcoming is part of keeping it useful.

We expect everyone taking part — issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews — to:

- **Be respectful and constructive.** Critique the data or the code, not the person.
- **Argue from evidence.** Disagreements about an entry are settled with primary sources, not volume or status.
- **Assume good faith.** Most "wrong" entries are honest mistakes or terms that changed. Correct them kindly.
- **Keep it on-topic and professional.** No harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, or spam.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment of any kind, publishing others' private information, sustained disruption, and deliberately submitting false or misleading data.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces — the repository, issues, pull requests, and discussions — and when representing the project in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Report concerns to the maintainer via the contact on their [GitHub profile](https://github.com/pacocartones), or through [GitHub's private reporting](https://github.com/pacocartones/free-llm-api-hub/security/advisories/new). Reports are handled confidentially.

Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and comments that violate this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban any contributor for behavior they deem inappropriate, threatening, or harmful.
