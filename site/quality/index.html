Data quality

Verified share, freshness and unknowns, counted from the data. Nothing on this page is typed by hand.

How much of the dataset is verified, how fresh it is, and how many answers are known, unknown with a recorded reason, or not checked yet. Every number on this page is computed from providers.json when the site is built. Data of 2026-11-25; ages are counted to that day.

To fill a gap, use Data coverage: it lists every open item with the provider's own docs and a prefilled issue. How an entry is checked is in the methodology.

Freshness

An entry is fresh up to 60 days after its last verification, due for a re-check from day 61, and stale after 90 days (the SLA). Counted over verified entries. Oldest verification: 2026-11-14; newest: 2026-11-25.

Text and non-text providers

The project is about free text LLM APIs. By the inclusion criteria, providers whose modalities do not include text (speech, image and similar) stay at or under 35% of the dataset. The stricter is_text_llm: false also counts embeddings, rerank and OCR services that take text but are not chat models.
