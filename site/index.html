What this is

An open, MIT-licensed dataset of 94 free LLM and AI-model APIs: 47 ongoing free tiers and 47 trial credits, listed apart. It is independent: not affiliated with or sponsored by any provider on it.

How we verify

93 of 94 entries are verified: the free tier, its limits and its main catch were read on the provider’s own docs on a recorded date. The dates run from 24 Nov 2026 to 27 Nov 2026. 1 entry could not be confirmed from an official source and is marked ⚠, not hidden.

Free LLM API questions

Is there a free LLM API?

Yes. This dataset records 94 free LLM and AI-model APIs: 47 ongoing free tiers and 47 one-time trial credits, 93 of 94 verified against the provider's own docs. The first three in our recommended order are AI Horde, OVHcloud AI Endpoints and Kilo Gateway. To match one to your needs (no card, commercial use, a model family), use the free API finder.

Which free LLM API has the highest limits?

Providers count limits in different units (requests per minute or per day, tokens, credits), so there is no single ranking. Of the 26 verified ongoing free tiers for text LLMs, 7 publish a daily request cap; the highest are Google Gemini API (AI Studio) (10,000 requests a day), Groq (1,000 requests a day) and Requesty (200 requests a day). For the other 19, a daily cap is not stated; each provider page quotes the limits in the provider's words. Compare them in the renewing free tiers collection.

Which free LLM APIs need no credit card?

37 of 94 providers state that no credit card is needed; 16 ask for one, and for 41 it is not stated. The first three in our recommended order are AI Horde, OVHcloud AI Endpoints and Kilo Gateway. See the no-credit-card collection and the guide to free LLM APIs without a credit card.

Can I use a free LLM API commercially?

It depends on the provider. On the free offer, 15 allow commercial use, 7 allow it with conditions, 13 are for evaluation only, and for 59 it is not stated. The first three that allow it, in our recommended order, are OCR.space, Speechify API and Veryfi. See the commercial-use collection and the guide to choosing a free LLM API for production.

Which free LLM APIs are OpenAI-compatible?

55 of 94 providers are OpenAI-compatible: you change the base URL and the key in the OpenAI SDK. 1 are not, and for 38 it is not stated. The first three in our recommended order are AI Horde, OVHcloud AI Endpoints and Kilo Gateway. See the OpenAI-compatible collection and the guide to OpenAI-compatible free APIs.

How fresh is this data?

93 of 94 entries are verified: the free tier, its limits and its catch were read on the provider's own docs on a recorded date. The verification dates run from 24 Nov 2026 to 27 Nov 2026. Every entry is due for a re-check 90 days after its date. 1 entry could not be confirmed from an official source and is marked, not hidden. See what “verified” means and what changed.
