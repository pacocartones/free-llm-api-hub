Questions

Which providers train on my data?

Each provider says it in its own policy. This page shows a provider's own sentence where one is recorded, with its page and the day it was read. Of 67 verified providers, 15 have a recorded sentence that says they do not train on prompts, 11 have one that says they may, and 41 are not confirmed.

What does “not confirmed” mean?

No sentence from the provider's own pages is recorded for the row. It never means “no”.

Does a row cover every plan?

A row covers the plan its sentence is read for, and the note says which when the page does. Other plans are not covered by that row.

Where do the sentences come from?

A recorded sentence is copied as written from the provider's own page, with the page and the day it was read.

How can I correct or add a row?

Use the link in the row to open an issue with the page and the sentence.

To add a sentence or fix one, read how we take a value (opens in a new tab) and open an issue from the row.
